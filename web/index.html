Safe, predictable sessions

  • One local workspaceThis server binds only to 127.0.0.1 and locks its database against another web server.
  • Resume where you left offQueue order, patient IDs, visits, and the active consultation are restored on restart.
  • Session-only undoUndo records reset when you restart or reload. Completed and missed visits are never undone.
  • Keep a backupWith the app stopped, copy the database to a safe location. Files are not encrypted.
